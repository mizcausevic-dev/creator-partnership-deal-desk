# Origin

Creator operations are not just a relationship problem. They are a brief, obligation, and launch problem. The moment a deal changes, teams need a surface that maps partnership drift to approvals, deliverables, and the next safe move.

We built `creator-partnership-deal-desk` to make that operating layer explicit. The point is not to replace a CRM or sponsorship platform. The point is to show what a Social / Creator Economy operator surface should look like when the audience needs to manage partnership pressure, downstream obligations, and launch posture with real revenue consequences attached.
